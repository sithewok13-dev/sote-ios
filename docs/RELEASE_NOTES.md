# Shadows of the Empire for iOS

### What's in v0.9.5

Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire on iPhone, with touch controls. You bring the game: the app has no ROM in it.

**Thank you, GTTeancum.** This port exists because of [SotE-Recomp](https://github.com/GTTeancum/SotE-Recomp), GTTeancum's PC port of Shadows of the Empire. When asked, GTTeancum opened its code to everyone and welcomed ports like this one. Its game code, menus, modern controls and cheats are what this app runs. Thanks also to the [N64Recomp](https://github.com/N64Recomp/N64Recomp) and [RT64](https://github.com/rt64/rt64) teams, whose recompiler and renderer make it possible.

**Sorry about the camera.** Photos and bursts work, but selfies, Action Freeze (swipe down) and the cinematic burst (swipe up) are still being worked on: the view can come out in the wrong place, show copies of Dash, or be one flat colour. They will be fixed in a later build.

### Install

1. Download `SotE-iOS-0.9.5.ipa` below and install it with Feather, SideStore, AltStore or Sideloadly.
2. Copy your own Shadows of the Empire ROM (USA, v1.2 / Rev 2) into Files > On My iPhone > SotE. Any name works, and so does a .zip with the ROM inside.
3. Open SotE. It checks the ROM on first launch.

### Controls

- Left thumb: a stick appears where your thumb lands and moves and strafes Dash. Drag anywhere else to look.
- A, B and the C buttons on the right, Z under L, L and R at the top.
- START: tap for Start. Hold it until its border fills for the tray: CONTROLS (Modern or the N64's Classic), GYRO, SENS, OPACITY, CAMERA, CHEAT, LEVEL SELECT (with the cheat on), D-PAD and SELFIES. The app's version shows just above the tray.
- CAMERA (top middle): tap for a photo, slide sideways for a burst. Photos go to your library, or to Files > SotE > Screenshots.
- CHEAT (turn it on in the tray): hold it to switch infinite health, lives, ammo, missiles and vehicle health. It also puts Dash back on solid ground after a bottomless fall.
- In the game's menus: tap a row to pick it, swipe up, down, left or right for the D-pad. The D-pad shows while a menu is up.
- A game controller works too.

### Known limitations

- The camera's selfies, Action Freeze and cinematic burst (see above).
- Now and then the whole screen can flash one colour for a frame.
- Only the USA v1.2 (Rev 2) ROM works.
